# 01 DISCOVER — Is This Problem Real and Worth Solving?

> **Version**: 1.0.0
> **Dominant Lenses**: Empathy + Validation
> **Duration**: 3-4 weeks typical (3-6 weeks range)
> **Exit**: Gate 1 Review

```
DISCOVER Lens Dominance:
  ██████████ EMPATHY      — Feel the user's pain. Every decision starts with user evidence.
  ██████░░░░ VALIDATION   — Frame hypotheses. Define what "true" looks like before testing.
  ██░░░░░░░░ SPEED        — Present but secondary. Resist building too soon.
  ██░░░░░░░░ GOVERNANCE   — Light structure. Research plan, not heavyweight process.
```

## Deliverables Checklist

Everything the team must produce before Gate 1. Track status in `PROGRESS.md`.

- [ ] **Research Plan** — Documented objectives, methods, timeline, and participant list
- [ ] **User Interviews** — Minimum 5 completed and synthesized (target 8-12)
- [ ] **Empathy Maps** — At least 1 per persona candidate (typically 2-3)
- [ ] **Validated Personas** — At least 1 primary persona validated against real data
- [ ] **Problem Statements** — Scored >= 7/10 on frequency + severity
- [ ] **Assumptions Register** — Minimum 15 assumptions documented and risk-ranked
- [ ] **Competitive Analysis** — Landscape documented with positioning map
- [ ] **Lean Canvas** — Draft reviewed for business model viability
- [ ] **Research Synthesis** — Reviewed by CEO and CPO
- [ ] **Hypothesis Cards** — Next-phase hypothesis cards drafted for top 3 problems
- [ ] **Gate 1 Evidence Package** — Compiled and ready for review

## Exit: Gate 1 Review

When all deliverables are complete, proceed to the Gate 1 Review.

**Gate 1 Location**: [06_GOVERNANCE/](../06_GOVERNANCE/)

**Gate 1 Criteria** (all must be met for GO):

- Minimum 5 user interviews completed and synthesized
- At least 1 primary persona validated
- Problem statement scores >= 7/10 average
- Assumptions register has 15+ entries, risk-ranked
- Competitive landscape documented
- Research synthesis reviewed by CEO and CPO
- Hypothesis cards drafted for top 3 problems
- Lean Canvas draft reviewed

**Gate Decisions**: GO | CONDITIONAL GO | NO-GO | PAUSE

**After Gate 1 GO --> proceed to [02_DESIGN/README.md](../02_DESIGN/README.md)**
