## Document Purpose

This register captures all key assumptions underlying the IRIS project — Samsung DSR's next-generation resource planning platform built by Amoza on Mendix 10 + Oracle 19c + Elasticsearch 8.x. IRIS is a NEW BUILD replacing PM Planner across five Samsung DS sites (Hwaseong, Pyeongtaek, Austin, Xi'an, Giheung).

Each assumption is scored for risk (Impact x Uncertainty) and prioritized for validation. Per the AX Framework, assumptions scoring >= 20 are flagged as **"Leap of Faith"** and must be validated before proceeding past the DISCOVER phase gate (Gate 1).

This CXO-authored version emphasizes user experience, adoption, and empathy-lens assumptions alongside technical and business dimensions.

## Scoring Guide

| Score | Impact (1-5)                                   | Uncertainty (1-5)                               |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Negligible — minor inconvenience               | Very confident — strong direct evidence         |
| 2     | Minor — workaround exists                      | Somewhat confident — some supporting evidence   |
| 3     | Moderate — timeline or cost affected           | Neutral — limited or indirect evidence          |
| 4     | Major — significant scope or timeline impact   | Uncertain — little direct evidence              |
| 5     | Catastrophic — project failure or cancellation | Highly uncertain — no evidence, pure assumption |

**Risk Score** = Impact x Uncertainty (Range: 1-25)
