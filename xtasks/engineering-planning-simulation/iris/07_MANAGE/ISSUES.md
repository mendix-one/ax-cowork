### Priority Definitions

| Priority      | Definition                                                                 | Response Time   | Resolution Target      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---------------------- |
| 🔴 **High**   | Blocks phase progress, affects gate readiness, or impacts production users | Same day        | Within 2 business days |
| 🟡 **Medium** | Impacts team velocity or quality but does not block progress               | Within 2 days   | Within 1 sprint        |
| 🟢 **Low**    | Minor inconvenience, improvement opportunity, or cosmetic issue            | Within 1 sprint | Best effort            |

## Issue Template

When adding a new issue, include:

```
| # | [Short description] | [Priority] | [Phase] | [Owner] | [YYYY-MM-DD] | ⬜ Open |
```

**Details** (add as a comment or sub-section if needed):

- **Description**: What is happening?
- **Impact**: What is affected? Who is blocked?
- **Steps to reproduce** (if applicable): How to observe the issue
- **Root cause** (if known): Why is this happening?
- **Proposed resolution**: What should be done?
